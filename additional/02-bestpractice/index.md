# Best Practices

Welcome to the Best Practices section. These guides are compiled from real-world testing scenarios to help you optimize your Mendix applications, design durable test strategies, and maintain test suites efficiently.

### Key Pillars

*   [**Testing Strategy**](bestpractice/start-testing-strategy) — Define your test scope, plan for unit vs. process testing, and establish a roll-out plan.
*   [**Application Optimization**](bestpractice/optimize-mendix-app) — Design your Mendix models to be highly testable by MTA.
*   [**Recording Actions**](bestpractice/record-actions) — Best practices for recording actions and completing test steps.
*   [**Data Maintenance**](bestpractice/maintain-test-data) — Strategies for preparing and maintaining stable test data.

---

:::tip
Adhering to these practices early in your testing lifecycle will save hours of maintenance as your Mendix application evolves.
:::
