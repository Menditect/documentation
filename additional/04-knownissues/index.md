# Known Issues & Workarounds

This section documents issues that customers have encountered, along with identified workarounds or official resolutions. 

While many of these issues are on our radar to be resolved directly in MTA, some stem from platform limitations or external variables. 

### Browse by Category

*   [**Design Tests Issues**](knownissues/design-tests) — Issues encountered during test creation, such as recorder failures.
*   [**Installation & Connection Issues**](knownissues/installation) — Issues with initial setup or agent-to-server connection.
*   [**Test Run Issues**](knownissues/test-runs) — Troubleshoot failures encountered during active test executions.
